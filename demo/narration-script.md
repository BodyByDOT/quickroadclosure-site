# QuickRoadClosure Demo deck: narration script

Voice: edge-tts `en-AU-NatashaNeural`, rate -8%. Music bed: "Proud of You" by Michael Ramir C. (Mixkit 1117, Mixkit Stock Music Free License), about 18 dB (roughly 13% level) under the voice, looping.
Sound is off by default; the nav **Sound** button (or the M key) turns it on. Each clip starts when its slide becomes active (or is replayed) and stops when you leave the slide.
Placeholders on the slides ([PRICE], [AMOUNT], [NAME], [DATE] and the like) are never read aloud.
Where the spoken text differs from the written line (acronyms spelled out, URL said as words), the "Spoken as" line shows what the voice was given.

## 07. Sight-distance warnings
Audio: `assets/audio/demo-07.mp3`

Hills, curves and brush can hide your signs. The app checks sight lines while you plan, warns you when a sign is too close for the speed, and suggests a fix, like moving it upstream.

## 08. In the field: sight lines
Audio: `assets/audio/demo-08.mp3`

Check the sight line before you set it. If a hill crest hides a sign, move it upstream, and once the crew confirms it from the field, everyone gets the change.

## 09. Share
